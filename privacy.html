PRIVACY

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

This is a personal portfolio site. It does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect personal information from you directly. The sections below explain the limited data that is involved when you visit or get in touch.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how it is used — for example, which pages are viewed, an approximate region, and the type of device and browser. IP addresses are anonymized. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this. No personally identifying information is collected or sold through analytics.

If you prefer not to be measured, you can enable "Do Not Track" in your browser, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Contact

If you reach out through the WhatsApp link or by email, that conversation and any information you share is handled by WhatsApp or your email provider under their own privacy policies. I use those messages only to respond to you.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. As part of serving the site, Vercel may log standard request metadata such as your IP address and user agent, in line with Vercel's privacy policy.

What this site does not do

There are no third-party advertising networks on this site, and your data is not sold to anyone.
