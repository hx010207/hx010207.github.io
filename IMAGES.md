### Tips
- Project images can be real screenshots, architecture diagrams, or clean terminal shots —
  anything that reads at a glance. Dark screenshots look great in the cyber (dark) theme.
- Square portrait works best for the round hero stickers.
- If you don't have a workspace photo, any clean desk / code-on-screen shot works.
